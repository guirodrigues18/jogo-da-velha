Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe Rules

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe, also called Tic-Tac-Toe 2.0 or Super Tic-Tac-Toe, is tic-tac-toe with 9 boards inside one big board. The rules take two minutes to learn, but every move becomes a strategic decision.

▶ Play now, free

The board

The big board has 3 × 3 squares, and each square is a regular tic-tac-toe mini-board. That makes 81 squares in total. One player is ✕, the other is ◯, and ✕ always goes first.

Goal

Win 3 mini-boards in a row on the big board: horizontally, vertically or diagonally. It's classic tic-tac-toe, except each big square has to be won first.

How to win a mini-board

Get 3 in a row inside it, exactly like regular tic-tac-toe. The mini-board becomes yours and is closed: nobody plays on it again.

The golden rule: your move picks your opponent's board

The square you play inside a mini-board decides which mini-board your opponent must play on next. Played in the top-right square? Your opponent has to play on the top-right mini-board.

✕ played in the top-right square of a mini-board → ◯ plays on the top-right mini-board.

That's what makes the game so interesting: every move is both an attack on your board and a choice of where your opponent plays.

What if the target board is closed?

If your move sends the opponent to a mini-board that has already been won or drawn, they get a free move: they can play on any mini-board that is still open. The first move of the game is also free.

Draws

  • A full mini-board without 3 in a row is drawn and counts for nobody.
  • If every mini-board closes and nobody makes the big line, the game is a draw.

Move timer

Each player has 20 seconds per move. If time runs out, the turn passes to the opponent, who plays on the same board you were supposed to play on. The clock warns you in the last 5 seconds.

You can let the clock run out up to 3 times per match. The 4th time, you lose the match. Your remaining chances show up as little dots (⏱ ●●●) next to the clock.

Game modes

  • 2 players on the same phone or computer.
  • Against the AI, on three levels: Easy, Medium and Hard. You choose to play as ✕ or ◯.
  • Daily challenge: a new position every day, the same for everyone.
  • Online: create a room and send the link to a friend. Other people can watch.

FAQ

Who goes first?
✕ always goes first, and the first move can be on any square of any board.
Can I play on a mini-board that has already been won?
No. Won or drawn boards stay closed until the end of the game.
Does a drawn mini-board count for anyone?
No. It doesn't count for either player when making a line on the big board.
Do I need to sign up?
No. It's free and runs right in the browser, on your phone or computer.
